# Changelog

## 2.1

- Editable IDT conditions for temperature, Na⁺, Mg²⁺, oligo, dNTPs,
  suboptimality and maximum foldings.
- 2' sugar icons in the hairpin, homodimer and linear views.
- One-click hairpin, self-dimer and Analyzer property results. The lowest-ΔG
  hairpin and homodimer are shown together with Tm, molecular weight,
  extinction coefficient, nmol/OD260 and µg/OD260.
- Homodimer drawing uses two antiparallel rows with counted and additional
  base pairs distinguished.
- Base-pair lines distinguish Watson–Crick, G·U/G·T wobble, other
  noncanonical identities and unknown pairs. The drawing does not infer 3D
  Hoogsteen/sugar-edge geometry, and does not draw G-quadruplex, i-motif,
  triplex or pseudoknot connectivity.
- 147 tests.

## 2.0

- IDT hairpin prediction with native SVG stem-loop, tree and arc layouts.
- Linear schematic, modification tags, sugar icons and legends.
